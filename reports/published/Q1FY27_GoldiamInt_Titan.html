Financial Results Deep-Dive — Goldiam International · Consolidated
Other income needs to be separated from the operating story: Of Goldiam's ₹363.7 Cr total income this quarter, ₹326.0 Cr was Revenue from Operations and ₹37.6 Cr (~10.3%) was Other Income — described by the company as "mainly tariff refunds." That refund flatters the reported 28.6% EBITDA margin and 858 bps YoY expansion. Management's own steady-state disclosure (ex-refund) puts underlying EBITDA margin expansion at a still-healthy ~400 bps YoY to ~24% — genuine, but noticeably smaller than the headline number.
The underlying margin driver is still structural: Even at the cleaner ~24% steady-state level, Goldiam's margin is driven by its US-origin raw-gold casting strategy (casting in the US, polishing/setting in India), which delivers a genuine "Product of Origin" that mitigates tariff exposure while protecting margins — a durable advantage as long as the tariff regime persists, separate from this quarter's specific refund.

Business Segments — Goldiam International
Core B2B Export Franchise

Manufactures and exports fine diamond jewellery (natural and lab-grown) to large global retailers, department stores and wholesalers, overwhelmingly US-focused. This remains the steady cash-generating backbone of the business.

Backward Integration — Eco-Friendly Diamonds

In-house diamond-growing subsidiary (Eco-Friendly Diamonds LLP, ~88% owned) gives Goldiam full backward integration into LGD supply — a structural cost and quality advantage over pure-play jewellery assemblers.

ORIGEM — Domestic B2C Retail

ORIGEM is Goldiam's India retail push into lab-grown diamond jewellery, positioned in the ₹40,000-50,000 everyday-wear price band with IGI-certified stones, lifetime buyback/exchange, and a Digital 3D Ring Builder for bespoke customisation. Now 26 stores across West, North and South India.

US-Origin Casting Strategy

Raw gold is cast in the US, with polishing and diamond-setting completed in India — establishing a genuine "US Product of Origin" that mitigates tariff exposure, protects margins, and supports US employment optics with American clients.

Business Analysis — Goldiam International · Research analysis only, not a recommendation
1. Split the reported margin beat from the refund-driven boost
The headline 858 bps margin expansion is partly a one-off tariff refund sitting in other income (₹37.6 Cr, ~10.3% of total income). Management's own steady-state disclosure still shows a genuine ~400 bps structural improvement (to ~24%) from the US-origin casting strategy — real, but about half the headline number.
Read With Care
2. Small base means high growth rates are easier to sustain — for now
At ~₹364 Cr quarterly total income, Goldiam is a fraction of the size of large-cap jewellery peers — 54% YoY growth is impressive but starts from a small base, and the law of large numbers will eventually apply.
Read With Care
3. LGD is now existentially central to the business
90.7% of export sales are lab-grown diamond jewellery. This is a high-conviction, high-payoff bet on the LGD category's continued global growth — and a concentration risk if LGD demand or pricing were ever to disappoint.
Key Swing Factor
4. ORIGEM is still small relative to the core business
26 stores generating a modest revenue contribution (₹170 Mn in all of FY26) versus a ₹1,000+ Cr annual base — ORIGEM is a credible second growth engine in the making, but not yet a needle-mover on group financials.
Watch Closely
5. Client and geography concentration is a real risk
Heavy reliance on US retail clients for the B2B export business means US consumer demand, tariff policy and retailer wallet-share decisions all have outsized influence on Goldiam's results.
Key Swing Factor
6. Balance sheet strength gives strategic optionality
Almost debt-free with a growing cash pile — Goldiam can self-fund the ORIGEM retail rollout and weather short-term tariff/gold-price volatility without needing external capital, a genuine advantage for a small-cap exporter.
Structural Positive

Neutral / Monitor

Neutral / Monitor
5. Gold-price and duty-policy sensitivity is the biggest swing factor
A single customs-duty change moved PBT growth by ~27 percentage points (64% vs 37% underlying) this quarter alone — Titan's near-term earnings remain unusually exposed to government gold-policy decisions and gold-price cycles.
Key Swing Factor
6. Scale and category leadership remain the core long-term moat
India's dominant organised jewellery retailer with 3,680+ stores group-wide, continued category leadership, and multiple growth levers (studded ratio, wedding-jewellery mix, international) support a durable multi-year growth story independent of any single quarter's noise.
Structural Positive

Key Analytical Takeaway

Both companies delivered genuinely strong Q1 FY27s, but they sit at opposite ends of the jewellery value chain. Goldiam is the higher-margin, faster-percentage-growth, tariff-hedged export specialist — small enough that a single ₹60 Cr order win is newsworthy, but structurally advantaged by its US-casting strategy and near debt-free balance sheet. Titan is the scale leader — a diversified consumer conglomerate whose Jewellery, Watches, Eyecare and CaratLane businesses all grew together this quarter, commanding by far the richer valuation multiple (~76× vs ~26-27× P/E) as the market prices its brand moat, distribution scale and multi-decade compounding track record. Goldiam's ORIGEM retail bet is, in effect, a small-scale attempt to build exactly the kind of B2C brand and store network that Titan has spent decades establishing at massive scale.
