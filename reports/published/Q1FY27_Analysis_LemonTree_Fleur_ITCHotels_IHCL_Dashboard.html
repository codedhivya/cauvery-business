Fleur Hotels is preparing a separate listing by end-2027Fleur Hotels 2027 end-குள்ள தனியா list ஆகப் போகுது

Lemon Tree is splitting into an asset-light brand and management company (Lemon Tree) and an asset-heavy owner and developer (Fleur). Fleur builds and owns hotels; Lemon Tree lends its brands and runs them for a fee.

Lemon Tree ரெண்டா பிரியுது: brand + management பார்க்கற asset-light Lemon Tree, hotel வாங்கி கட்டி சொந்தமா வச்சிருக்கற asset-heavy Fleur. Fleur கட்டும், Lemon Tree தன் பேரைக் கொடுத்து fee வாங்கி நடத்தும்.

  • Warburg Pincus has committed up to ₹960 crore of primary equity; with about ₹300 crore of internal accruals, Fleur has about ₹1,300 crore of equity available now.
  • Plan: deploy about US$1 billion over 4 to 5 years on land, new hotels and hotel acquisitions.
  • Fleur today: 24 operating hotels, 3,993 keys. After Lemon Tree’s owned hotels transfer: 41 hotels, 5,813 keys. Plus about 2,500 more rooms planned, which points to roughly 8,300 to 9,000 keys by FY30†.
  • Listing: management now says by end-2027 (Q1 FY27 results said second half of 2027; an August report had said first half of 2027).
  • Warburg Pincus ₹960 crore வரை primary equity போடுது; ₹300 crore internal accruals சேர்த்து இப்போவே ₹1,300 crore equity ready.
  • 4–5 வருஷத்துல சுமார் US$1 billion — land, புது hotel, hotel acquisition-க்கு.
  • இன்னிக்கு Fleur: 24 hotel, 3,993 keys. Lemon Tree-யோட owned hotels வந்ததும்: 41 hotel, 5,813 keys. அதுக்கு மேல ~2,500 rooms — FY30-குள்ள சுமார் 8,300 முதல் 9,000 keys†.
  • Listing: இப்போ management சொல்றது 2027 end-குள்ள (Q1 FY27 results-ல 2027 இரண்டாம் பாதின்னு சொன்னாங்க; ஆகஸ்ட் report முதல் பாதின்னு சொல்லிச்சு).

      Demand and supply driversDemand, supply driver

      • Rates and occupancy: IHCL like-for-like domestic RevPAR +14%; Lemon Tree occupancy 75.7% (+314 bps) but average room rate only +2%; ITC Hotels room revenue +8%. Volume is strong; pricing power differs by segment.
      • Events and weddings: ITC Hotels cited a high base in MICE and weddings last year; brokers expect H2 FY27 recovery if geopolitics eases.
      • Tax: GST changes and loss of input credit cost Lemon Tree about 2.3% of margin in the quarter, mitigated by price actions.
      • Shocks: the quarter began with demand disruption linked to the West Asia conflict and air-travel disruption.
      • Supply: every chain is signing aggressively. Lemon Tree signed 13 hotels (1,020 rooms) in the quarter; IHCL 20 signings and 11 openings; ITC Hotels 8 signings. Most new keys are asset-light, so they add fees, not capital.
      • Rate, occupancy: IHCL domestic RevPAR +14%; Lemon Tree occupancy 75.7% (+314 bps) ஆனா room rate +2% தான்; ITC Hotels room revenue +8%. Volume நல்லா இருக்கு, pricing power segment-ஐப் பொறுத்தது.
      • Wedding, MICE: போன வருஷம் base உயரம்னு ITC Hotels சொல்றாங்க; geopolitics சரியானா H2 FY27-ல recovery-னு brokers எதிர்பார்க்கறாங்க.

      Investment angle (education, not advice)Investment பார்வை (education மட்டும்)

      • Three different bets on the same demand: Taj (scale, brand premium, highest multiple), ITC Hotels (almost debt-free, F&B-heavy, low return on equity so far), Lemon Tree plus Fleur (mid-priced leader, leverage falling, structural change pending).
      • Fee income earns far higher margins than owning, which is why markets pay up for asset-light growth. Lemon Tree’s long-term fee margin target is 70 to 80%; Fleur’s owned-hotel margin target is about 55 to 56%.
      • Ownership is where cycles bite: rent, interest and depreciation sit under EBITDA. Lemon Tree’s interest cost was ₹159 crore over the last twelve months against ₹699 crore operating profit.
      • ஒரே demand மேல மூணு வேற bet: Taj (scale, brand premium, அதிக multiple), ITC Hotels (கடன் கிட்டத்தட்ட இல்ல, F&B அதிகம், return on equity இன்னும் குறைவு), Lemon Tree + Fleur (mid-price leader, கடன் குறையுது, structure மாற்றம் pending).
      • Fee income-க்கு margin ரொம்ப அதிகம், அதனால market asset-light growth-க்கு premium தருது. Lemon Tree fee margin target 70–80%; Fleur owned hotel margin target ~55–56%.
      • Cycle கடிக்கறது ownership-ல: rent, interest, depreciation EBITDA-க்கு கீழ. Lemon Tree interest கடந்த 12 மாசம் ₹159 crore, operating profit ₹699 crore.
