Key takeaways

  • Price: record highs in Sep 2026 on mine accidents (Grasberg, Escondida), Chilean output falls and US tariff stockpiling; slight pull-back since.
  • Supply vs demand debate: ICSG (30 Jun 2026) projects a small surplus of 96 KT in 2026 and 377 KT in 2027; banks (Goldman, Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Wood Mackenzie) see deficits of 330–640 KT.
  • Raw-material squeeze: concentrate is so tight that treatment charges went negative; smelters survive on sulphuric acid (~US$400/t) and precious-metal by-product credits.
  • EV & grid: a battery EV uses ~83 kg copper vs ~23 kg in a petrol/diesel car; grids are the single biggest long-term driver; AI data centres add ~1 MT by 2030.
  • India: refined output ~0.7 MT in FY26 (+25%), heading to ~1.2 MT by FY30 — still a 0.8–1.1 MT shortfall.
  • Capex wave: HCL ₹7,200 cr (3× ore), Hindalco ₹2,450 cr Dahej + recycling, Adani Kutch Phase 2 (→1 MTPA by Mar 2029), Vedanta Saudi rod mill.

Smelter margin maths (why negative TC/RC hurts)

Smelter margin =
  TC/RC received (now −US$100/t)
+ By-product credits (acid ~US$400/t, gold, silver)
+ Free metal (recovery > payable %)
+ Rod/tube conversion premium
− Power, fuel, labour, freight

Miner (HCL) instead earns: LME price − mining cost − TC/RC paid. When TC/RC turns negative, the miner gets more than the metal value of its concentrate. That is why HCL's margin jumped to 54%.

Demand drivers: grid, EV, data centres — and the India gap

EV-க்கு petrol car-ஐ விட 3.5 மடங்கு copper வேணும். Grid தான் பெரிய demand. இந்தியா 96% import பண்றோம்.

Copper per vehicle (kg)

International Copper Association / IDTechEx. Fast chargers add up to 8 kg each; a standard charger ~0.7 kg.

India refined copper output FY26 (million tonnes)

BigMint. Total ~0.7 MT (+25% YoY). Imports FY26 ~0.51 MT. HCL now sells concentrate, not cathode.

EV impact — the real size

  • BEV 83 kg vs ICE 23 kg → +60 kg per car; e-bus 224–369 kg.
  • EV demand was projected to rise from 185 KT (2017) to 1.74 MT (2027) by ICA/IDTechEx.
  • But the bigger copper story is grid build-out (transmission, transformers, renewables) — EV is the third driver after grid and renewables.
  • Risk: aluminium substitution in wiring harnesses and LFP/thrifting can trim per-vehicle copper.

AI & data centres

  • ~1 MT extra demand by 2030 — "modest against a 40-million-tonne market".
  • Grid expansion and hardening called "the single most important driver over the next couple of decades".
  • Industry needs ~900 KT of new mine supply every year.
Source: Mining Weekly, 4 Jun 2026

India demand–supply

  • Demand grew 9.3% in FY25; ICRA sees 10–12% growth over the next two years.
  • FY30 India refined output ~1.2 MT; shortfall 0.8–1.1 MT persists.
  • Copper Vision Document (Jul 2025): demand 6× by 2047; add 5 MTPA smelting/refining by 2030.
  • Sector CAGR to FY30: transport/EV 18%, buildings 17%, infra/industrial 12%, durables 6%.
Sources: Outlook Business; ICRA; BigMint; Ministry of Mines
Is copper really the "new gold"? Partly. Gold is a store of value; copper is a consumable whose price depends on supply discipline and industrial demand. The "new gold" tag fits the 2026 scarcity premium, but copper still behaves like a cyclical commodity — ICSG's 2027 surplus forecast (377 KT) is the reminder.
Gold-ஐ சேமிச்சு வெப்போம்; copper-ஐ பயன்படுத்துவோம். அதனால copper விலை cycle-ல ஏறும், இறங்கும் — அத மறக்கக் கூடாது.

BSE 513599 · NSE HINDCOPPER Hindustan Copper Ltd

Business model: India's only vertically integrated copper miner (Govt of India, Ministry of Mines). Mines ore at Malanjkhand (MP), Khetri/Kolihan (Rajasthan) and the Indian Copper Complex (Jharkhand), concentrates it, and sells copper concentrate (metal-in-concentrate, MIC) priced off LME. Smelting was suspended earlier; the focus since 2020 is mining.

HCL = mine owner. விலை LME-ல ஏறினா நேரடியா லாபம் ஏறும். Smelting இப்போ இல்ல, concentrate விக்கறாங்க.

NSE HINDALCO Hindalco Industries — Copper division (Birla Copper, Dahej)

Business model: India's largest custom copper smelter at Dahej, Gujarat (~0.45 MT output FY26). Buys imported concentrate, earns TC/RC + by-product credits (sulphuric acid, phosphoric acid/DAP fertiliser, gold, silver) + premiums on continuous-cast (CC) rods, copper tubes and inner-grooved tubes (for ACs). Copper ≈ 20% of group revenue but ~6% of EBITDA; aluminium (India) and Novelis dominate.

Working capital ↔ CFO link: When copper prices rise, smelters (Hindalco, Adani) must fund costlier concentrate and stock — inventory days rise and CFO lags PAT. A miner like HCL sells concentrate quickly (debtor days 16) and has little purchased raw material, so higher prices flow straight into cash. That is why HCL's CFO (₹1,474 cr) exceeded its PAT in FY26 while Hindalco's did not.

Risks & CB educational verdict

முக்கிய risk-கள் — அப்புறம் CB-யோட பாடம் (recommendation இல்ல).

Key risks

  • Price cycle: copper behaves like a commodity; ICSG sees surplus in 2026–27. Miners (HCL) have the highest operating leverage.
  • Concentrate scarcity: custom smelters (Hindalco, Adani, Vedanta) cannot pass on negative TC/RC.
  • Geopolitics: Hormuz-type closures hit sulphur, acid and freight; Brent spikes raise smelting cost.
  • Policy: US tariffs, China export bans (acid), India import duty or QCO changes.
  • Execution: HCL's 3× capacity plan depends on clearances, MDO partners and a new CMD.
  • Substitution: aluminium replaces copper in some cables and EV harnesses when copper is expensive.
  • Rupee: copper is dollar-priced — a weaker rupee lifts HCL's realisation but raises smelters' input bills.

Verdict (education only)

Copper is "new gold" for the miner, not for every copper company. The scarcity premium in 2026 sits at the mine. Among Indian listed names, only Hindustan Copper is a direct, high-margin play on the copper price — and its share price has already de-rated from the peak as the cycle matured and the government sold stock.

Hindalco and Adani are converters: they win on volume, by-products and value-added products, and would actually benefit if TC/RCs normalise even as copper prices cool. Vedanta's copper is a small, low-margin part of a zinc–silver story.
