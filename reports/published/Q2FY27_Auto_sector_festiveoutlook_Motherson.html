Auto Sector: the Festive Runway

Record August passenger vehicle dispatches, a softer two-wheeler mix, and five tailwinds lining up before Navratri–Diwali. Ancillaries, EV, CNG & ethanol read-through included.
Published 4 Sep 2026
Educational note · not investment advice

August 2026 was a record August for passenger vehicles — roughly 4.5 lakh units dispatched, up ~40% on a weak year-ago base, just 1.7% shy of July's own record. Two-wheelers grew for most large OEMs too, but the growth segment within 2W — electric scooters — cooled 16% month-on-month after a subsidy cut, and premium motorcycles (350cc+) slipped. Retail momentum around Onam (+61% in Kerala) points to a strong festive stretch ahead.

Five tailwinds are converging on the same quarter — three of them squarely consumer-facing, two structural. None is a one-off; each still has months of runway left.

TailwindWhat's happeningAuto read-through
Festive season Onam retail already up 61% YoY in Kerala; Navratri & Diwali (Oct–Nov) traditionally carry the year's heaviest retail volumes, with dealer bookings (e.g. Brezza: 55,000+ in 35 days) running ahead of supply. Demand pull
GST 2.0 rate cut Two-wheelers up to 350cc and small cars moved from 28% to 18% GST, effective 22 Sep 2025 — up to ₹24,500 cheaper on a 2W. A full year on, the lower price point is now the base, not a one-time pop. Price advantage
Income-tax relief Nil tax up to ₹12 lakh (₹12.75 lakh for salaried) has been in salaried pockets since FY26 and was left untouched in the FY27 Budget — more disposable income available for discretionary, big-ticket purchases. Disposable income
Indigenisation thrust At the Sept'26 SIAM convention, Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal warned OEMs against "nominal" 10–15% value-add passed off as local content, and flagged IEC-level tracking two supply-chain levels down. Favours real localisers
Clean-grid milestone Solar + wind briefly supplied a record ~42.8% of grid load in mid-July 2026, on the back of 100+ GW combined capacity — part of the same indigenisation push now extending to batteries, motors and EV charging infra. EV ecosystem signal
Net-net: two of the five tailwinds (GST, tax relief) are now base-effect rather than fresh triggers — the incremental swing factor into Diwali is festive footfall plus how convincingly OEMs and ancillaries respond to the localisation push.

CNG

Maruti Suzuki's CNG sales jumped 40–58% through 2026, with a 9-lakh-unit target for FY27. Seven of India's ten best-selling CNG cars are Marutis — the segment is now a genuine growth engine, not a niche.

Ethanol

India hit its E20 blending target nationwide in 2025 — five years ahead of the original 2030 goal. Policy focus has now shifted to E27/E30 and flex-fuel vehicles, trimming the crude import bill and supporting farm incomes.

How Motherson makes money

SAMIL is a Tier-1 global systems supplier, not a single-part maker — it designs and builds whole modules (wiring harnesses, mirrors, bumpers, dashboards, polymer assemblies) that OEMs bolt straight onto the line. Growth has come from a deliberate "diworsification" strategy: spreading revenue across auto and non-auto, ICE and EV, and dozens of OEMs and geographies, so no single platform or powertrain decides its fate.

Two engines built the group: organic capacity expansion in existing plants, and M&A/JV-led entry into new capabilities (mirrors via SMR, polymers & modules via SMP, truck wiring via PKC). It now runs manufacturing across 41 countries and is extending the same playbook into non-auto — aerospace, health & medical, IT and logistics — under its "Emerging Businesses" bucket. Promoter (Samvardhana Motherson group) holds roughly 48.6% of the company.

Analyst view

Educational summary, not a recommendation — CB Research does not issue buy/sell/hold calls.

Q1 FY27 is a story of margin discipline under pressure: Motherson pushed through one of its sharpest commodity-cost quarters in years without giving up EBITDA margin, while continuing to de-lever and diversify away from pure auto-cyclicality. That is a genuinely strong operating result. The counterweight is valuation — at ~38x trailing earnings, much of this good news already appears priced in. The variable worth tracking over the next few quarters isn't the growth rate, which has been consistently strong, but whether margin can keep expanding if input costs (copper, aluminium, polymer, freight) stay elevated for longer than pass-through clauses can absorb.

The setup into Navratri–Diwali is constructive: a record PV base, gas and ethanol both scaling smoothly, and EVs compounding off a small number — but two watch-items deserve attention rather than the festive noise.
