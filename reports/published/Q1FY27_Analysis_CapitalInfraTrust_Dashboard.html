Tax Flow – where tax is charged (general guidance)

Tap a layer – SPV, Trust or Unitholder.
Section 80M vs the InvIT pass-through
  • Section 80M relieves a domestic company that receives dividend and passes it on to its own shareholders. It only avoids double tax at the holding-company level; the shareholder is still taxed on the dividend. It covers dividend only, not interest or capital repayment.
  • The Trust is not a company. Its relief comes from the separate business-trust pass-through rules for SPV interest and dividend, subject to conditions.
  • So “Trust profits are not taxable” is loose: SPV profit is taxed at SPV level, and the final tax is collected from unitholders.
General guidance only – not tax advice. Rates, conditions, TDS and the treatment for each class of unitholder should be confirmed with the Trust's tax counsel or a chartered accountant. The deck does not disclose SPV-wise tax paid or effective tax rates, so none are estimated here.

SWOT

Strengths

  • Sovereign-linked NHAI annuities, no traffic risk
  • AAA rating; net debt/EV 41.1%
  • Fixed-price O&M via sponsor PMA; no NHAI litigation or penalty
  • Cost of debt falling (7.33% → 7.24%)
  • Diverse base: MFs 17%, insurance 11%, pension 4%

Weaknesses

  • FY27 DPU guide ~20% below FY26
  • Lumpy half-yearly annuity timing makes quarters uneven
  • Sponsor ~32% holder and also project manager and ROFO seller – related-party structure
  • Residual life 12.9 yrs: NAV amortises without acquisitions
  • Q1 consolidated opex +88% QoQ

Opportunities

  • 6 ROFO assets (₹48,710 Mn) → 18 assets; AUM +50% to ~₹100,000 Mn
  • 17-asset ROFO pipeline; third-party assets possible
  • NMP 2.0: ~₹3.35 lakh crore highway monetisation; road InvIT AUM ₹3.2 tn (2026) → ₹8.8 tn (2030E)
  • Higher leverage (to 57.5–60%) can lift DPU if acquisitions are accretive

Threats

  • Rate-hike cycle / inflation (FY27 inflation forecast 5.1%; repo 5.25%)
  • Acquisition valuation or funding risk; unit dilution
  • Leverage creep toward 60–70%
  • Slower NHAI payments or annuity deductions
  • Policy/tax changes on InvIT distributions
