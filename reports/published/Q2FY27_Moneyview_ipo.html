Route A · Partner-funded (off-book)Route A · Partner funding (off-book)

  • Bank/NBFC lends; Moneyview earns origination + servicing fees.Bank/NBFC loan கொடுக்குது; Moneyview-க்கு origination + servicing fees.

Route B · Own NBFC (Whizdm Finance)Route B · சொந்த NBFC (Whizdm Finance)
