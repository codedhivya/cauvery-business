🔋 Why Q1 FY27 Is the Inflection Quarter

Q1 FY27 (April–June 2026) is the quarter both Exide and Amara Raja moved from "gigafactory under construction" to "gigafactory dispatching samples." Exide's Bengaluru NCM cylindrical line sent out its first locally-manufactured cell samples; Amara Raja commissioned its ₹500 Cr Customer Qualification Plant in Telangana. Neither company has booked meaningful Li-ion cell revenue yet — both remain lead-acid earnings stories for now, with Li-ion as a funded, de-risking option.

Exide Energy Solutions (EESL) — Bengaluru
6 GWh Phase 1 · Scalable to 12 GWh
Construction & Certification — Done
100% of equipment across all four production lines delivered and installed; 100% of utilities fully operationalised. BIS and UN 38.3 product certifications completed during the quarter — the regulatory green light needed before any commercial dispatch.
Samples — Started
First NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt) cylindrical line commenced customer sample deliveries — the first locally-manufactured cells from the plant. The LFP prismatic line began sample supplies for 3-wheeler and telecom applications. Commercial cell revenue still targeted for later in FY27, after OEM qualification cycles complete.

Cumulative equity in EESL: ₹4,902 Cr (₹100 Cr infused July 2026). FY27 board-approved capex: ₹1,400 Cr. Target: 25–30% capacity utilisation in Year 1.

🟢
Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies (ARACT) — Giga Corridor, Telangana
16 GWh Target by FY30 · ₹9,500 Cr Total Plan
Customer Qualification Plant — Commissioned
₹500 Cr Customer Qualification Plant (CQP) at Divitipally commissioned 15 July 2026, inaugurated by Telangana CM A. Revanth Reddy. Initial capacity 60 MWh; reconfigurable for cylindrical and prismatic cells across multiple chemistries (LFP + NMC). 100+ employees. Customer sample validation begins from August 2026 — one quarter behind Exide's first dispatch.
Technology Partner — Now Named
ARACT holds an LFP technology licensing agreement with GIB EnergyX, a subsidiary of Slovakia-based Gotion High-Tech — resolving the "no named partner" gap flagged in the Q4 FY26 dashboard. Separately, a July 2026 partnership with Nuvation Energy (US) covers localising high-voltage battery management systems (BMS) in India.

₹150 Cr infused into ARACT in Q1 FY27; cumulative funding ~₹1,900 Cr. Flagship Giga Cell plant (16 GWh) targets operations from Q2 CY2027, starting at 2 GWh NMC. Separate 10 GWh BESS Giga facility planned for grid/C&I storage.

🔵 Exide Industries — CB Verdict Q1 FY27
Exide's Q1 FY27 print did what it needed to: it converted "gigafactory optionality" from a slide-deck promise into a dispatched-samples reality, while the core lead-acid business kept compounding at double-digit growth with expanding margin. Zero debt, ₹655 Cr EBITDA (+19.5% YoY), and 27%+ PAT growth give the company a strong platform to fund the remaining ramp without diluting shareholders or adding leverage.

The key investment question shifts, but doesn't disappear: samples are not revenue. OEM qualification cycles typically run several months, and management's own guidance is for commercial cell revenue to arrive later in FY27 — not this quarter. Whether cylindrical cell quality holds up through full OEM validation, and whether India's Li-ion demand curve scales fast enough to matter before Chinese import cells set the price ceiling, are still open questions the market has partly pre-paid for via the post-result rally to a 52-week high.

CB Research Assessment: Execution has been consistent and the balance sheet gives real optionality. The stock's re-rating has captured much of the "samples dispatched" milestone already — the next test is booked commercial revenue, not just plant readiness.
🟢 Amara Raja E&M — CB Verdict Q1 FY27
AMEM's Q1 FY27 tells a familiar two-track story: standalone revenue growth accelerated to +20.6% YoY, its fastest pace in several quarters, while EBITDA margin compressed further to roughly 9.6% — extending a trend now running close to seven quarters. The market's reaction (a ~2.9% sell-off on results day) suggests investors are, correctly, weighting the margin trajectory over the topline print.

On the strategic side, Q1 FY27 answered two of the biggest open questions from the Q4 FY26 dashboard: the Customer Qualification Plant is commissioned, and a named technology partner — GIB EnergyX, a Gotion High-Tech subsidiary — is now on record for the LFP cell chemistry. That materially de-risks the execution narrative around the ₹9,500 Cr Giga Corridor plan, even if it doesn't yet move the margin needle.

CB Research Assessment: At roughly 17.6x trailing PE, the market continues to price in margin risk more than growth optionality. The Giga Corridor de-risking this quarter is a genuine positive, but the earnings algebra won't improve until EBITDA margin stabilises — that remains the single variable to track into FY27.
