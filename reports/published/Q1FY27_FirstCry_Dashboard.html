FirstCry (Brainbees Solutions): Q1 FY27 Business & Investment Analysis

Results for the quarter ended June 2026, the journey since the August 2024 IPO, segments & model, EBITDA/net-profit reality check, SWOT and peer comparison with the 9 new-age listed companies covered earlier

Overview: Is FirstCry EBITDA-positive? Net-profitable?

Short answer: FirstCry is EBITDA-positive (reported EBITDA ₹59.65 crore, 2.8% margin; company-defined Adjusted EBITDA ₹89.3 crore, 4.24% margin) but not yet net-profitable on a consolidated basis — Q1 FY27 consolidated loss after tax was about ₹44 crore (narrowed 34% YoY from ₹66.5 crore). The parent-company (standalone) entity did earn a profit (₹21.6 crore) and the India multi-channel business was PAT-positive; the losses come from the International (UAE/KSA) business and other consolidated items. Roughly ₹47 crore of the quarter's operating cushion appears to come from other income, so on an ex-other-income basis the consolidated loss would be about ₹90 crore (CB Research approximate working, flagged below).

What stands out

  • Growth re-accelerated: revenue +13% YoY, the strongest in five quarters; India multi-channel +~18% (best in seven quarters).
  • Margins are under pressure even as growth improves: gross margin fell to 36.5% from 38.5%; Adjusted EBITDA margin slipped to 4.24% from 4.98% (diapering competition, rupee depreciation and crude-linked raw material costs, per management).
  • Globalbees (brand aggregator) delivered Adjusted EBITDA up 308% YoY; International losses (Adjusted EBITDA) reduced about 22% YoY.

Business Segments & Model

FirstCry (Brainbees Solutions, founded 2010) is India's largest multi-channel retailer for mothers', babies' and kids' products — apparel, footwear, diapers, toys, baby gear, nursery and personal care, up to age 12 — sold through its website/app and FirstCry/BabyHug modern stores (company-owned and franchised).

Business model

  • Category focus creates high repeat: management cites an average of 8.0 transactions by Year 4 of a cohort and 10.5 by Year 8.
  • Vertical integration through own brands raises gross margin (mix ~ mid-30s %), but exposes the company to raw-material and manufacturing costs.
  • Omnichannel: online plus modern stores; offline GMV grew ~15% YoY in Q1 FY27; net addition target of about 90–100 stores in FY27.
  • Delivery speed as a moat: RocketBees in 72 cities (over half of online shipments); Qwik in 12 cities (~125,000 shipments in June 2026).

Stores & footprint (dark stores / physical)

  • Modern stores: 1,190 as of 31 March 2026 (company-owned and franchise); Q1 FY27 count not confirmed — flagged n/a.
  • Unlike Blinkit-style dark stores, FirstCry's quick-delivery uses in-house delivery networks and store/warehouse hubs; a separate dark-store count is not disclosed — n/a.
  • Annual unique transacting customers: 11.8 million (+10% YoY); India multi-channel 11.3 million.

Why the stock fell

  • Persistent consolidated losses and no clear date for net profitability, while peers such as Nykaa have moved to profits.
  • Gross-margin compression (diapering competition, input costs) and India multi-channel margin decline despite better growth.
  • Slower-than-hoped International (UAE/KSA) turnaround because of promotional intensity from large horizontal e-commerce entrants.
  • Large pre-IPO investor lock-in expiries and share overhang typical of new-age IPOs (general pattern; not specifically verified for each date).

Where FirstCry stands

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • India's largest multi-channel mother-baby-kids retailer; category specialist with high repeat behaviour
  • Omnichannel reach: ~1,190 modern stores plus app/web; 11.8 million annual transacting customers
  • Own-brand portfolio and vertical integration support gross margin in the mid-30s
  • Improving working capital (NWC days 57, inventory days 85) and positive free cash flow in FY26
  • Globalbees now covering fixed costs; Adjusted EBITDA up 308% YoY

Weaknesses

  • Still consolidated loss-making (Q1 FY27 ₹44 cr; FY26 about ₹140 cr)
  • Gross margin down 200 bps YoY; Adjusted EBITDA margin down to 4.24%
  • International (UAE/KSA) still loss-making after several years
  • Meaningful reliance on other income: ex-other-income loss roughly double the reported loss (approximate)
  • Low ROE (~1.8%) and premium P/E on thin earnings

Opportunities

  • Net addition of ~90–100 modern stores planned in FY27 plus offline portfolio realignment
  • RocketBees/Qwik faster delivery to counter quick-commerce entrants
  • Globalbees listing as a value-unlocking exit route
  • Gulf expansion incl. first Riyadh store; management expects gross-margin recovery from Q2 FY27

Threats

  • Quick-commerce and horizontal players (Blinkit, Zepto, Amazon, Flipkart, Nykaa-style category extensions) targeting baby categories
  • Diapering price wars; rupee depreciation and crude-linked raw-material costs
  • India's birth-rate trends over the long term (risk flagged by brokers)
  • Physical-store expansion may cannibalise existing footprint (flagged by a broker at IPO)
