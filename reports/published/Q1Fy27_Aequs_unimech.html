  • Both trade near 13× book value; markets are pricing years of growth.
  • Fresh equity coming: Aequs ₹650 cr promoter warrants; Unimech up to ₹750 cr QIP.
  • Aequs: aerospace 40% வளர்ந்திருக்கு, segment margin 23%; consumer பக்கம் இன்னும் loss (EBITDA –₹36 cr).

Aequs

Contract manufacturer with two engines

ரெண்டு engine கொண்ட contract manufacturer

  • Makes aircraft parts to customer drawings (build-to-print) for Airbus, Boeing, Safran, Collins.
  • Only Indian maker doing forging → machining → surface treatment → assembly inside one SEZ (Belagavi).
  • Consumer arm: toys (Mattel), cookware (Tramontina), consumer-electronics parts for a large global brand (Hubballi).
  • 88% of revenue is exports; 99% of raw material is imported.
  • Earns per part shipped; order book = long-term agreements × aircraft build rates.
  • Customer கொடுக்குற drawing படி aircraft parts செய்யறாங்க (build-to-print) — Airbus, Boeing, Safran, Collins-க்கு.
  • Forging → machining → surface treatment → assembly எல்லாமே ஒரே SEZ-க்குள்ள (Belagavi) செய்யுற ஒரே இந்திய company.
  • Consumer பக்கம்: toys (Mattel), cookware (Tramontina), ஒரு பெரிய global brand-க்கு consumer electronics parts (Hubballi).
  • Revenue-ல 88% export; raw material-ல 99% import.
  • ஒவ்வொரு part ship பண்ணும்போதும் வருமானம்; order book = long-term agreements × aircraft build rate.

Unimech

Specialist tool-maker becoming a parts platform

Tool-maker-லிருந்து parts platform-ஆ மாறுது

  • Core (76%): aero tooling — engine lifting beams, transport stands, assembly jigs used by GE, Rolls-Royce, Airbus, Boeing and MRO shops.
  • Low volume, high mix (2,500+ SKUs) → ~65% gross margin.
  • New legs: precision components & assemblies (aerostructures via FACC, semiconductor, nuclear for NPCIL).
  • Hobel Bellows (bought Apr 2026, ₹450 cr): metallic bellows & expansion joints, ~90% export.
  • Saudi JV with Kanoo (51%): local manufacturing, production from Mar/Apr 2027.
  • Core business (76%): aero tooling — engine-ஐ தூக்குற beam, transport stand, assembly jig; GE, Rolls-Royce, Airbus, Boeing, MRO shops use பண்றாங்க.
  • Volume கம்மி, variety அதிகம் (2,500+ SKUs) → gross margin சுமார் 65%.
  • புது பாதைகள்: precision components & assemblies (FACC வழியா aerostructures, semiconductor, NPCIL-க்கு nuclear).
  • Hobel Bellows (Apr 2026-ல ₹450 cr-க்கு வாங்குனாங்க): metal bellows, expansion joints; சுமார் 90% export.
  • Kanoo கூட Saudi JV (51%): அங்கேயே manufacturing, Mar/Apr 2027-ல production.
